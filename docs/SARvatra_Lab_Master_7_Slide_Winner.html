Creativity — The Dual-Regime SAR Breakthrough

Replacing cloud-blocked optical imagery and single-threshold water masks with physics-exact microwave scattering.
⏳ Traditional Flood Mapping
  • Optical camera blindness: Sentinel-2 and Landsat optical sensors yield 0% visibility in cloud, heavy rain, or night passes.
  • Single-threshold backscatter drops: Detects only open calm water, completely missing flooded tea gardens and forests.
  • Cloud-dependent dashboards: Freeze and fail when rural cellular towers lose power during regional emergencies.
⚡ SARvatra Dual-Regime Radar Physics
  • 9.3 cm S-band microwaves (3.2 GHz): Microwave pulses pierce heavy tropical rain clouds and darkness 24/7.
  • Dual-Regime Scattering: Mathematically separates Specular Water (σ° < −16 dB) from Sub-Canopy Double-Bounce (Δσ° > +3.0 dB).
  • Offline-First Boat Mode: Caches vector maps, elevation transects, and rescue routes locally in IndexedDB with zero internet.
Key Innovation: We transformed flood response from cloud-dependent guesswork into an instant, physics-exact, zero-internet radar command solution.
