Creativity – The Radar-AI Breakthrough

⏳ Traditional Flood Mapping
  • Optical sensor blindness: Thick rain clouds and night passes result in zero usable imagery.
  • Static single-thresholding: Produces heavy false alarms from calm water look-alikes and smooth asphalt.
  • Manual GIS bottlenecks: Days of manual vectorization before emergency responders receive maps.
⚡ SARvatra Dual-Band Physics Fitting
  • NISAR S-Band (3.2 GHz) + Sentinel-1: Microwave pulses pierce torrential rain & cloud cover 24/7.
  • Copernicus 30m DEM slope fitting: Hydrodynamic depth transect separates true flooding from terrain shadows.
  • Dijkstra dry-corridor search: Real-time road graph routing finds elevated escape corridors in <0.5s.
  • 100–500× faster than conventional manual GIS workflows.

Key Innovation:

We've transformed multi-gigabyte raw SAR radar backscatter from an opaque research file into an instant, math-exact rescue navigation and evacuation solution accessible on low-cost field devices.
