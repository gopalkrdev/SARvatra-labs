2 The Scientific Breakthrough: 2 Radar Scattering Mechanisms

Most amateur flood projects look only for "dark pixels". SARvatra Lab models two distinct physical scattering regimes to reveal what optical cameras and simple thresholds miss completely:

1. Specular Reflection (Calm Standing Water → Cyan Map Overlay) Δσ° < -3.5 dB

The Simple Analogy: Standing water acts exactly like a flat glass mirror in a dark room. When the satellite radar shoots microwaves down at the floodwater, the smooth water surface reflects the energy forward and away into outer space.

The Physics Signature: The satellite antenna receives virtually zero returned signal, resulting in a catastrophic backscatter collapse below -16.0 dB (dropping as low as -18.9 dB in the Dikhow confluence). SARvatra flags this as Open Inundation (12,337 Hectares).

2. Dihedral Double-Bounce (Flooded Rice / Submerged Stems → Magenta Overlay) Δσ° > +3.0 dB

The Simple Analogy: Think of a corner mirror (like bicycle reflectors). In agricultural fields, mature Sali paddy rice stalks stand upright while 2 to 3 feet of water covers the roots.

The Physics Signature: The S-band 9.3 cm microwave penetrates through upper leaf foliage, hits the horizontal water surface, bounces sideways into the vertical plant stem, and rebounds straight back into the satellite antenna!

Instead of decreasing, the backscatter SURGES by +3.4 dB! Optical satellites see green leaves and report dry ground. SARvatra Lab unveils 13,105 Hectares of Submerged Paddy Crops!
